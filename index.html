Questions

Before you request.

Clear expectations make premium travel easier.

Does TAKE ME LUXE operate aircraft, yachts or vehicles directly?

TAKE ME LUXE is presented as a request and coordination platform. Specific transportation is intended to be fulfilled by appropriately licensed third-party operators and providers.

Can I request more than one service in the same journey?

Yes. The request form supports multi-service journeys, such as a private jet plus ground transfer and yacht arrangement.

Is submitting a request a confirmed booking?

No. A request is not a confirmed reservation or contract. Availability, pricing and final terms would be confirmed separately.

Which destinations are available?

The site is designed for worldwide request intake. Actual service availability depends on the relevant third-party provider and destination.
